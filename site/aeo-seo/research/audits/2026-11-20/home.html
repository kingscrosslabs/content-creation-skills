What are content creation skills?

A skill is a folder with a SKILL.md file that teaches your AI agent one job, step by step. Install it once and your agent knows when to use it.

These skills teach it your content workflow. Before writing a word, they read a small content/ folder about you: your voice, your pillars, your reader and the posts that worked. So drafts sound like you, not like a chatbot, and you stay the author.

  1. 01

    Install the skills

    One command adds them to Claude Code, Codex, Cursor or any agent that reads SKILL.md.

  2. 02

    Build your content folder

    content-setup interviews you, links files you already have, or drafts from your past posts. It writes a content/ folder every skill reads first.

  3. 03

    Ask for a post

    “Give me hooks for a reel about my morning routine.” “Turn this newsletter into X posts.” Your agent picks the right skill and stops for your approval.

FAQ

How do I use AI to write social media posts that sound like me?+

Give the AI your voice before you ask for a post. These skills read a content/ folder about you (how you talk, your topics, your reader and your best-performing posts) every time they write, so drafts start from your voice instead of a generic one. You still approve every draft.

Can Claude write Instagram Reels and TikTok scripts?+

Yes. short-form-video-hook gives you 10 opening lines for a topic, short-form-video-script turns the one you pick into a talking-head script under 60 seconds, and short-form-video-caption writes the caption. They run in Claude Code, Codex, Cursor and other agents that load skills.

How do I write a good hook for Reels or TikTok?+

Lead with the payoff or a claim that cuts against what your viewer believes, in under two seconds. short-form-video-hook writes 10 options for one topic in two styles: a tutorial hook that promises a specific result, or an authority hook that pairs borrowed credibility with a contrarian take.

How do I turn a newsletter into social media posts?+

Pull the strongest standalone ideas out of the issue and rewrite each as its own short post that links back. short-form-text-from-newsletter does this for X, Threads and Substack Notes from one newsletter issue.

How do I find content ideas when I'm stuck?+

Start from what you already did, said and saw this week, not a blank page. content-ideas reads your recent notes, drafts and saved links, scores each idea against your topics and reader, and sorts them by format. content-planning then turns them into a two-week calendar.

How do I set up an Instagram comment-to-DM automation?+

dm-automation sets one up through LedaChat: people comment a keyword on your post, get a DM, can be asked to follow and leave their email, then get your link. Write your default DM copy once and each new automation only needs the post and the link.

What are agent skills?+

Agent skills are folders with a SKILL.md file that teach an AI agent how to do one job. The agent reads each skill's short description and loads the full instructions only when a task needs them. Claude Code, Codex, Cursor and other agents support the format.

How do I install the content creation skills?+

Run npx skills add kingscrosslabs/content-creation-skills and pick the skills you want, or copy the folders into your agent's skills folder (for Claude Code, .claude/skills/). Then run content-setup to build your content folder.

Can these skills schedule and publish my posts?+

Yes, but only after you approve. content-publish posts or schedules content to Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, YouTube, LinkedIn, X and Bluesky through Adeli, and asks before every post. content-wins then pulls your stats so future drafts learn from what worked.

Are the content creation skills free?+

Yes. They're open source under the MIT licence. The publishing, stats and DM skills use Adeli and LedaChat, which both have free plans.
