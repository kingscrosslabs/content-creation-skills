# Experience bank

The first-hand material only you can put on a page: what you did, what you saw, your own numbers, and quotes from people you talked to. It's what makes a page worth citing over the ten others answering the same query. The page skills pull from it instead of writing vague "when we…" lines.

**Rules**
- **Only `approved` entries go on the site.** A person on your team approves an entry by writing it, or by changing `pending` to `approved`. An agent never approves an entry.
- **Specifics live here and nowhere else.** A page can name dates, counts or outcomes only when an approved entry states them. Otherwise it stays general.
- **Data needs its date and method.** Product stats are aggregate and anonymized, with the period and how they were pulled. Survey results give the question, sample size, where it ran and when.
- **No customer names** unless the customer agreed in writing.
- One fact per entry. If a fact changes, edit the entry and its date, and update the pages that use it.

**Entry format**

```
- [story|data|survey|quote] <the fact, in a sentence or two> · source: <who> · <YYYY-MM-DD> · <approved|pending> · used in: <slug>, <slug>
```

- **story:** something you did or saw yourselves
- **data:** an aggregate stat from your product
- **survey:** a result from a poll you ran
- **quote:** something a named person told you directly, with their OK to quote it

Group entries under topic headings that match the query bank's clusters.
