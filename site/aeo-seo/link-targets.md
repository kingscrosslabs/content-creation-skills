# Link targets

Places where the site can get a listing, a backlink or a mention: directories, review sites, curated lists, newsletters and roundups your buyers read. Link pitches come from here, and new targets get added at the bottom of their section.

**Rules**
- `- [ ]` means not done, `- [x]` means submitted or sent, with the date: `(sent 2026-01-12)`.
- When a pitch is drafted, add `(pitch drafted YYYY-MM-DD)` so no target gets pitched twice.
- **Priority:** P1 = this week · P2 = this month · P3 = later.
- **Free first.** Mark paid listings `PARKED · paid` instead of deleting them. No link farms.
- **Never pitch a competitor**, or a list a competitor publishes.

**Line format**

```
- [ ] **<Name>** (P<n> · free|paid · dofollow?) — <submit URL> — <category, audience, what it needs>
```
