# Competitors

Who searchers compare you against. Comparison pages and listicles read this before writing, and update it when they find a fact has changed.

**Rules**
- Every fact has a source URL and the date it was checked. A fact older than 90 days gets checked again before it goes on a page.
- Facts only, from the competitor's own pricing page, docs or changelog. No opinions, no customer feedback.
- The site never links to a competitor. Source URLs are for you, not for the page.
