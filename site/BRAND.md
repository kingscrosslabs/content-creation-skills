## Strategy

### What it is

A set of open-source agent skills that write short-form video scripts,
captions, X and Threads posts, LinkedIn posts, carousels and newsletters in the
user's own voice. They run in Claude Code, Codex, Cursor and any agent that
reads `SKILL.md` files. They plan, write, publish (through Adeli) and learn from
what performed.

They are the skills King's Cross Labs uses every week for Mika's own content.

### The problem

AI writing tools produce posts that sound like AI writing tools. The fix people
reach for (a long prompt pasted into a chat) forgets everything by the next
session, knows nothing about what worked last month, and still sounds generic.

### Audience

**Primary:** founders, marketers and creators who post on social and already
use an AI agent. They have a point of view and a backlog of ideas, and not
enough hours to turn them into posts every week.

**What they believe:** consistent posting compounds, and AI should be able to
help without making them sound like everyone else.

**What they're skeptical of:** "AI content" that reads like AI content, ghost
writing that loses their voice, and tools that post without asking.

**Not for:** anyone who wants a fully automated content farm or wants to post
under someone else's voice.

### Positioning

**Category:** open-source agent skills for creating content.

**Differentiators**

- **Your voice, from your files.** Every skill reads a `content/` folder about
  you first: voice, pillars, reader, point of view.
- **It learns from your winners.** Your best-performing posts are filed by
  format and read by the skills for that format.
- **Format first, platform second.** The format sets the rules; each skill
  knows the platform details.
- **You stay the author.** Hooks, outlines and drafts wait for your approval.
  Nothing posts without a yes.
- **Free and open source.** MIT licence.

### Personality

Direct. Practical. On the creator's side. Builder to builder.

It is not: a growth hack, a viral-formula seller, or a ghostwriter that
replaces you.

## Voice

Follows the KCL company voice: direct, sharp, warm when it's earned, never
corporate, never over-hedged.

### Rules for this brand

- Say what the skill makes (a hook, a script, a caption) before why it helps.
- Name the real platforms: Instagram, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, LinkedIn, X,
  Threads, Substack. Vague "social channels" is banned.
- Talk about the reader's voice, not "brand voice" or "tone of voice settings".
- Short sentences. Colons over dashes. No exclamation marks.
- Never promise virality, followers or growth numbers.

### Vocabulary

| Use | Avoid |
|---|---|
| in your voice | AI-generated, on autopilot |
| skills | prompts, templates, hacks |
| your agent | the AI, the bot |
| you approve | fully automated, set and forget |
| posts that worked, winners | viral content |
| open source, free | freemium, get started for free |

Banned outright: unlock, supercharge, game-changer, revolutionize, seamless,
leverage (as a verb), go viral, 10x your content.

### Taglines

- Primary: **Your voice. Your agent. You approve every post.**
- Hero: **Content creation skills for AI agents.**
- Short: **Open-source content skills for your agent.**

## Governance

### Naming

- The product is **Content Creation Skills** in titles and
  **content-creation-skills** as the repo and wordmark.
- Skill names are always written in code style and exactly as the folder:
  `short-form-video-hook`, not "Hook generator".

### Claims

- Never promise reach, followers or virality. Say what the skill makes.
- A skill's status on the site must match the README: Available, In progress
  or Coming soon.
- Agent compatibility claims must be true: only list agents that load
  `SKILL.md` skills.
- Adeli and LedaChat are named as what they are: the services the publishing,
  stats and DM skills use. They are KCL products; say so if asked.
- Credit: "Built by Mika Reyes at King's Cross Labs."

### Calls to action

Every primary CTA is **Star on GitHub** and links to the repo. No email capture,
no waitlist, no pricing.
